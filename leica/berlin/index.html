Berlin

YoungJun Koo reports from the streets of Berlin

The Pick up

After arriving in Berlin, Koo took the train to the Leitz Park in Wetzlar at the heart of Germany, where he picked up the Leica Q at the Leica Store.

Leica HQ

The world famous Leitz Park in not just the birthplace of each Leica camera but is also home to the Leica Archive, the Leica Gallery Wetzlar and the Leica Hall of Fame.

Natural Elements

By working with natural light and capturing the tones of natural world, the resulting visual language provides a strong framework for creating a coherent series of street shots.

Physical Forms
